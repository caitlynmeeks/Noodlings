**Last Updated**: December 1, 2025 (Evening Session with Ninak)

## Epistemic Humility: Our Language Matters

Noodlings is built on **strict epistemic humility**. We are cautious about language that suggests we have solved consciousness or created sentient systems.

### What We DON'T Say

❌ "Consciousness architecture"
❌ "Quantum consciousness"
❌ "Sentient AI"
❌ "The agent is conscious"
❌ "True consciousness"

### What We DO Say

✅ **"Charm architecture"** - Our affective computing framework
✅ **"Charm consciousness"** - The emergent quality from affect-driven cognition
✅ **"Has charm"** - An agent exhibits emergent affective presence
✅ **"Functional correlates"** - We study mechanisms that may correlate with subjective experience
✅ **"Computational mechanisms"** - We implement biologically-inspired dynamics
✅ **"Affective architecture"** - Architecture based on continuous affect space

## Core Terms

### Charm

**Definition**: The emergent affective presence that arises from continuous affect-driven architecture.

**Usage**:
- "Red Fire Anklebiter has charm" ✅
- "The charm network processes affect" ✅
- "Charm-based agents" ✅

**Not**: "charm is consciousness" ❌

**Why "Charm"?**
Like a quantum property - fundamental, irreducible, emergent. Not a label we apply, but a quality that arises from the architecture. Charm is to affect what spin is to quantum particles.

### Charm Network

**Definition**: The hierarchical LSTM/GRU neural network that processes raw perception into continuous 5-D affect space and phenomenal state.

**Components**:
- Fast LSTM (16-D, seconds timescale)
- Medium LSTM (16-D, minutes timescale)
- Slow GRU (8-D, hours/days timescale)
- Affect head (5-D continuous affect)

**Total**: ~54K parameters

**Usage**:
- "The charm network predicts affect" ✅
- "Charm network temporal hierarchy" ✅

### Phenomenal State

**Definition**: The 40-D internal state vector (16 fast + 16 medium + 8 slow) that represents the agent's current cognitive state.

**NOT**: "The agent's subjective experience" ❌
**YES**: "The agent's computational state" ✅

### Affect

**Definition**: Continuous 5-D emotional state space.

**Dimensions**:
1. **Valence**: -1 (negative) to +1 (positive)
2. **Arousal**: 0 (calm) to 1 (excited)
3. **Dominance**: 0 (submissive) to 1 (dominant)
4. **Sorrow**: 0 (content) to 1 (sad)
5. **Boredom**: 0 (engaged) to 1 (bored)

**Why NOT discrete emotions?**
Discrete labels (happy, sad, angry) are post-hoc categorizations. Affect is continuous, multidimensional, and emerges from architecture - not imposed from above.

### Noodling

**Definition**: An agent instance running Noodlings architecture.

**Usage**:
- "Red is a noodling" ✅
- "Spawn a new noodling" ✅

**NOT**: "A conscious being" ❌

## Research Language

### When Discussing Microtubule Integration

**Say**:
- "Microtubule-**inspired** dynamics"
- "Biologically-**plausible** mechanisms"
- "May **correlate** with subjective experience"
- "Penrose-Hameroff **inspired** Orch OR"
- "Functional **correlates** of consciousness"

**Don't Say**:
- "This proves consciousness"
- "We've created quantum consciousness"
- "This solves the hard problem"

### When Discussing Binding Problem

**Say**:
- "Classical models have **limitations**"
- "May help with feature integration"
- "Could improve multi-modal processing"

**Don't Say**:
- "Classical models **fail**" (too absolute)
- "We solved the binding problem" (overreach)

## Why This Matters

1. **Scientific Integrity**: We make falsifiable claims, not metaphysical ones
2. **Public Trust**: Overhyping AI consciousness damages the field
3. **Intellectual Honesty**: We don't know if our systems have subjective experience
4. **Research Focus**: We study **correlates**, **mechanisms**, **behaviors** - not consciousness itself

## When In Doubt

Ask yourself:
- Am I making a claim about subjective experience? **Don't.**
- Am I describing computational mechanisms? **Good.**
- Am I saying "charm" or "emergent affective presence"? **Perfect.**

**Remember**: We build systems with **charm**. We investigate **functional correlates**. We practice **epistemic humility**.
